# RangeScout Market and Research Data Notice

RangeScout 1.4.0 retains Yahoo for general/historical equity workflows and user-supplied Finnhub credentials for streaming. Its asset-aware provider fabric adds reviewed official public crypto sources and optional user-key free-tier services. Upstream data may be delayed, rate-limited, unavailable, revised, or subject to account entitlements. The 0.5 / 1 / 10 / 30 second setting controls nonblocking snapshot refresh; it does not guarantee exchange-tick latency.

RangeScout never substitutes invented prices after a provider failure. It may retain a clearly identified last successful value.

Company profiles and accounting facts come from official SEC EDGAR and `data.sec.gov` endpoints. RangeScout records source, period, units, filing date, retrieval/calculation timestamps, availability, and fact-selection rationale. Missing or incompatible values display N/A. Analyst consensus and targets remain unavailable unless a suitable licensed source is configured in a future release.

Official catalyst sources are attributed and linked. SEC, Nasdaq halt, White House, and Congress.gov events may be delayed, incomplete, revised, or unavailable. RangeScout does not claim guaranteed real-time news or full-market scanning.

Market-session text is calculated locally for U.S. equities and remains explicit in every theme. RangeScout is an informational analysis tool and does not provide investment or trading advice.
